1 Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Attendance Guru (referred to as "we", "us", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, handles, and protects information when you install and use our Android mobile application (Package Name: com.aistudio.attendanceregister.app).

The app is designed to assist small and medium businesses, contractors, shopkeepers, teachers, and team leaders in managing daily staff attendance, calculating overtime, recording advances, tracking daily and monthly wages, and exporting monthly reports.

2 Information We Collect and Manage

The App processes only the information you voluntarily enter into your attendance register:

  • Staff / Worker Profiles: Names, designations/roles, phone numbers, and authorization profiles (Administrator, Teacher, Student).
  • Attendance Records: Daily marking records (Present, Absent, Half Day, Holiday, Leave), dates, shift overtime hours, and optional shift notes.
  • Salary & Wage Configuration: Daily wage amounts, monthly base pay, cash advances, overtime pay rates, and calculated net earnings.
  • User Preferences: Dark/light UI mode, selected calendar month, and configured shift reminder times.

What We DO NOT Collect:

  • We do not collect background location or GPS tracking data.
  • We do not access your device contacts list or call logs.
  • We do not collect device IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, or advertising identifiers (AAID).
  • We do not process personal payment credentials, bank account passwords, or debit/credit card numbers.

3 Data Storage, Retention, and Security

1. Local Storage on Device: All attendance, staff, and wage records are stored inside a secure SQLite database powered by Android Jetpack Room. This database is sandboxed in your device's internal storage directory, inaccessible to other apps.

2. Cloud Backup (Optional): If you deliberately choose to use the cloud backup/restore feature, your register data is transmitted securely via TLS 1.3 encryption directly to your authenticated Google Cloud / Firebase project.

3. Data Retention: Records remain stored on your device until you choose to delete them within the App, or until you uninstall the App or clear storage through Android Settings.

4 Android Device Permissions

We declare and request only the absolute minimum permissions necessary to deliver the features you choose to use:

Permission Protection Level Purpose in Attendance Guru
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime (Android 13+) Sends scheduled shift reminders and daily attendance prompt alerts configured by the user.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Normal Triggers shift reminder alarms at the exact hour and minute selected by the user.
FileProvider Content URI Enables safe, secure sharing of generated PDF and CSV attendance reports via WhatsApp, Gmail, or Telegram without requesting broad storage permissions (zero-permission compliance).

Zero Broad Storage Access: In compliance with Google Play's privacy rules, this application does not request broad storage permissions (such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE).

5 Third-Party Services and AI Integration

The App integrates with select trusted third-party developer libraries to provide core functionality:

  • Google Play Services: Used for app distribution, updates, and core Android platform functionality.
  • Firebase AI Logic (Google Gemini 3.8 Flash): The App provides an optional "AI Invite" assistant to help users craft promotional invitation messages for colleagues. When tapped, generic promotional prompts are processed through Firebase AI Logic. No staff names, attendance logs, or financial figures are sent to or processed by the AI model.
  • Firebase App Check: Protects app backend integrations against abuse and ensures request authenticity.

We do not embed third-party advertising SDKs (such as AdMob, Meta Audience Network, or Unity Ads), and we do not track you across other applications or websites.

6 User Rights and Data Deletion

You have complete control over all data managed inside Attendance Guru:

  • Access & Review: View all staff members, attendance histories, overtime logs, and salary calculations at any time on the Register, Summary, and Reports screens.
  • Modification: Edit any attendance mark, daily wage, or worker profile directly with a single tap.
  • Data Portability / Export: Generate and export complete attendance and salary sheets in standard PDF or CSV format.
  • Deletion: Remove individual staff members or attendance records directly inside the App.
  • Permanent Erasure: To completely erase all data stored by the App, navigate on your device to:
    Settings > Apps > Attendance Guru > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.

7 Children's & Educational Privacy

When utilized by schools or teachers to monitor student attendance:

  • The App does not market to or profile children under the age of 13.
  • Student profiles and attendance logs are maintained strictly for educational record-keeping by the authorized instructor or administrator.
  • We comply with all Google Play Families policies and applicable international child data privacy regulations.
